Capacity accounting limits
The 128 B/function and 1024 B/module bounds are logical estimate bounds. A one-worker parallel join uses capacity for both the worker arena and final joined buffer; size-class rounding can increase allocated bytes. For memory, logical 496->624 should change two heap classes 512->640: approximately 256 extra bytes versus normal baseline, while avoiding two 896 B growth objects versus unreserved D. DR's 108 allocation-count test therefore does not imply equal allocated bytes.
Seqtk is on the large serial heap path: with at least 32 functions and mean body size below 2048, compile.go reduces capacity by25% when at least 256 KiB. Its logical 128 B addition becomes approximately 96 B of requested capacity before heap-class rounding, and may stay in the same class. If its measured B/op is unchanged, do not generalize this to every false-positive module or the maximum 1024 B hint. Only this control's actual byte/count/time results support its cost. Predictor audit is not a public allocation measurement for all 14 qualifying modules.
