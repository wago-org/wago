# Rejected stop-after-second-seed mitigation

The candidate disables seed encoding checks once two canonical static ADD X16 seeds are found. It was measured in four reversed100ms compile cross-build pairs against the same frozen final source, with loop-memory-base ON in both binaries. ADPCM -1.00% paired (-0.60% pooled), geo-point -2.30% (-1.27%), DCT -1.11% (-0.24%); regalloc +1.01% (+1.47%), Reed +0.77% (+0.78%), glob +0.26% (+0.62%). Three gains and three slowdowns, with ADPCM/DCT intervals crossing zero, do not establish a clearly useful aggregate tradeoff. The stopped scan removes much of the initial variant cost but offers no clear retained gain. It is rejected, and peephole.go is restored byte-for-byte from the original. No native-identity/oracle qualification was run. Raws, summary, source script and disabled patch are retained under compile-seed-preflight-stop-*.
