# ARM64 native import register entry

Retain Apple measurement source `fffbf964` against `73639628`. A runtime-verified scalar Go leaf receives a second entry 192 bytes after its unchanged dispatch wrapper. This entry accepts canonical i32 arguments in X0/X1 and returns canonical i32 in X0. Eligible import callers skip argument/result buffer pointers and home the returned value at the existing join. The native-leaf proof, clobber mask, context preservation, scheduler policy, ownership, and wrapper/tail ABI remain unchanged. Serialized artifacts continue to discard native admission proof.

The public probe initially suggested a 3.8% ordinary-thread improvement against the frozen source and 7.6% against its same-binary control. A second five-pair probe weakened this to 1.0% and 1.4%; compact imports were flat to slightly slower. Those results are preserved rather than selecting the favorable probe.

Actual wasm.fyi SDK fresh/reverse orders measure ordinary threads at 320/331 µs, 0.958/0.994 times baseline and 1.70/1.73 times pinned SIMD w2c2-clang `f303ed27`. Ordinary compact imports take 452/455 µs, 0.996/0.977 times baseline and 1.52/1.54 times peer. Opaque callbacks retain their Go transition and remain approximately 21.69 times peer; their source change is 1.010/0.995. All use the existing fixture, binding, entry API, performance(4096), and 64-repeat contract.

The complete 266-case pass has unchanged functional outcomes. Across 255 positive steady pairs, current/baseline is 1.00335; feature-only is 0.99996. Compile ratio is 0.99868 and compile RSS 1.00319. It remains isolated in a private experimental worktree for allocation/layout diagnosis and is not part of this source or publication.
